Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data FitnessHubb (the "App") collects when you connect your TikTok account, and how it is used.

Data We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, TikTok shares only the information covered by the permissions you approve, such as your basic profile information, account stats, video list, and videos you upload. The App never sees or stores your TikTok password.

How We Use Data

Your data is used only to operate the App for you: showing your videos and stats, and uploading or publishing content you choose. The App does not sell your personal data.

Sharing

Data is passed through TikTok's API and the integration service used to connect your account (Composio) solely to carry out your requests. It is not shared with any other third party.

Retention

Data is kept only while your TikTok account stays connected. When you disconnect the App or revoke its access in your TikTok settings, the App no longer has access to your account.

Cookies and Tracking

These pages set no cookies and run no trackers.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the FitnessHubb TikTok account.
